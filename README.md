## About Corabin

Corabin is a web design and development studio based in **Kolkata, India**, founded in **September 2026** by [Souvik Parya](https://corabin.com/about). We focus on one industry: **real estate**.

Agents, brokers, small developers and property managers often run their whole business through WhatsApp and social media. It works, until a buyer wants to browse listings, compare properties and send a proper enquiry. We build the website that makes that easy.

> **Mission:** make a professional web presence affordable for every real estate professional.

## How we work

| Step | What happens |
|:---:|---|
| **01** | **Understand.** We look at your business, your listings and who you want to reach. |
| **02** | **Design.** Layouts are planned around property showcase and lead capture. |
| **03** | **Build and test.** Development in Next.js, then checked on real devices. |
| **04** | **Launch and support.** We go live together and stay available afterwards. |

**Typical timeline:** 2 to 4 weeks. You deal directly with the founder, from the first message to launch. There are no account managers in between.
